# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard | Tableau

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard built using Tableau Public. It visualizes sales performance across different product categories, payment methods, genders, invoice dates, and shopping malls.

The dashboard helps users understand sales trends, compare category performance, and identify top-performing shopping malls through clear and interactive visualizations.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze sales performance across product categories.
- Understand customer payment preferences.
- Compare sales distribution by gender.
- Track monthly sales trends.
- Identify shopping malls generating the highest sales.
- Present key performance indicators (KPIs) in a single dashboard.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales by Category
A treemap showing sales distribution across product categories, including Clothing, Shoes, and Technology.

### 2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Displays key business metrics:
- Total Sales
- Total Quantity
- Average Order Value (AOV)

### 3. Sales by Payment Mode
A pie chart comparing sales distribution across:
- Cash
- Credit Card
- Debit Card

### 4. Sales by Gender
Visualizes sales distribution between male and female customers.

### 5. Sales by Invoice Date
A line chart showing monthly sales trends to help identify changes in sales performance throughout the year.

### 6. Sales by Mall
A horizontal bar chart comparing sales across shopping malls and highlighting top-performing locations.
